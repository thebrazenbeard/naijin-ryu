# Glossary and terminology normalization

**Primary source:** `Glossary.pdf`, 24 pages; secondary confirmation in `Naijin-Ryū Manual .docx`, the SURGE manual, and the True Ascension guides. Entries below are intentionally **concise, non-operational** definitions of concepts. Precise etymology and historic martial attribution require separate validation.

## The five martial pillars

**Shock** — immediate disruption and response (glossary p. 18). **Unbalance** — change in stability and base (p. 22). **Reversal** — redirection of incoming force (p. 15). **Grapple** — positional control in close contact or on the ground (p. 7). **Environment** — context, terrain and opportunities for escape or protection (p. 6).

## External martial and religious influences

The source glossary also defines or refers to **Aikidō, Judo, Sambo, Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, Muay Thai, Krav Maga, Kali, Silat, Systema, Wing Chun, Taijiquan, Kenjutsu, Kabbalah, Brahman, Maya, Moksha, Nirvana, fana, Ki and Kiai**. References indicate influence or comparison in an authored synthesis; they do not independently validate historical lineage, endorsement or exact equivalence.

## Naming cleanup (editorial only)

- Preferred display spelling: **Naijin-Ryū**; accepted search/URL alias **Naijin-Ryu**.
- Preferred rank spelling: **White-Flare**; **WhiteFlare** in some sources is an orthographic variant.
- **Phase 4 Guardian / Guardian's Path / Mastery & Teaching** are linked labels for an ongoing developmental teaching stage, not an automatic credential.
- **Sashes** refer to martial progression; **Aeons** refer to the spiritual narrative.
- The glossary refers to an **“Eclipse of Dawn Manual”** in citations, while an available advanced source is titled **“Clear Intent: Last Resort Combat Manual for the Kurai Tenka.”** Treat their equivalence as **unconfirmed**, not a silent renaming.

## Restricted term coverage

The original PDF includes many named lethal-force maneuvers and pressure-based incapacitation claims. Those entries are preserved in the original source only and are **not** expanded into public how-to instructions here. A glossary is not proof of the effectiveness or safety of any named movement.
