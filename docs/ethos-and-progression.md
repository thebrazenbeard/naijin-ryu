# Ethics, rank, and progression

**Source:** `Naijin-Ryū Manual .docx`, sections I–II. This page describes the author's stated system; it does not independently validate the system's outcomes.

## Four guiding principles

- **Guard Life, Not Ego.** Restraint, life preservation, and de-escalation take priority over ego, humiliation, or dominance.
- **Centerline Ascension.** Structural alignment and balance are taught alongside their symbolic connection to inner steadiness.
- **Shadow Integration.** Fear, aggression, and reactions are acknowledged rather than denied, so that responses may be intentional.
- **Skill as Sacrament.** Repetition, study, and service are framed as mindful practices; the dojo is described as a temple of learning.

## Sashes of Dawn

The manual names five ranks, with no black belt:

| Sash | Title | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Charcoal | Initiate | Ashes, potential, humility |
| Brown | Foundation | Earth, roots, stability |
| Green | Growth | Expansion and development |
| Blue | Sky | Wider understanding and responsibility |
| White-Flare | Ascendant | Compassion, service, mastery without ego |

Advancement combines technical proficiency, personal development, and teaching without injury. The manual discusses demonstrations and teaching evaluations; it does not document external certification.

The **Sashes of Dawn** are distinct from the **Six Aeons** of spiritual teaching. See [True Ascension](true-ascension.md).

## Force Ladder

The main manual, section VII, describes a staged ethical framework for deciding when to disengage, de-escalate, or use protective action. Its central principle is minimum necessary force and reconsideration when a threat ends. This page records that ethical model without reproducing injurious techniques or representing any use of force as medically harmless.
