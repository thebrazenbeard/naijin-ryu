# Four-session orientation to the complete Naijin-Ryū system

**Status: EDITORIAL READER/STUDY PATH, not a canonical initiation, rank requirement, church service or therapeutic practice.** The author's `NRY-023` foundation connects martial ethics, optional bodily practice, the True Ascension philosophy, reflective integration and possible community. Each session below gives a short *choice* of activities; none requires physical training, supernatural belief, trauma disclosure or another participant.

Sessions can be used separately, in any order, or not at all. Times are proposed reading windows, not source-authored ritual durations.

## Session 1 — the ethical center (15–20 min)

**Original sources:** `NRY-007` martial manual §I; `NRY-023` Church package §§1–2.

**Read:** [Four ethics](ethos-and-progression.md) and [integrative foundation](FOUNDATION.md).

**Try, optionally:** summarize **Guard Life, Not Ego** in a sentence that still means something when nobody is watching. Then distinguish the other named ethics—**Centerline Ascension, Shadow Integration, Skill as Sacrament**—without converting them into permission to control another person.

**Question:** What ethical restraint would matter *before* a physical or interpersonal dispute?

**Evidence limit:** explaining an ethos is not proof of adherence, physical ability or moral superiority.

## Session 2 — disciplined movement, with real choice (15–20 min)

**Original sources:** `NRY-007` §§II–IV and `NRY-013` student phases. Related: `NRY-009` mental conditioning.

**Read:** [Five SURGE pillars](surge.md), [curriculum alternatives](curriculum.md), and the [martial workstream](../programs/martial-training/README.md).

**Try, optionally:** identify the *principle-level* difference between Shock, Unbalance, Reversal, Grapple and Environment. In a comfortable clear space, you may practice one minute of easy walking and notice the environment; you may instead answer entirely in words. **Do not practice throws, chokes, locks, strikes to vulnerable targets or surprise attacks from a reading guide.**

**Question:** Which situations are better solved by leaving or asking for help than by applying a technique?

**Evidence limit:** a named pillar is a source's technical category, not field-tested efficacy. The **five Sashes of Dawn** are a different classification from the five SURGE pillars.

## Session 3 — a worldview that allows disagreement (15–25 min)

**Original sources:** `NRY-017` accessible True Ascension guide, `NRY-018` extended Guide, `NRY-023` §§1–2 and 8.

**Read:** [True Ascension](true-ascension.md), [the Six Aeons](spiritual/aeons-and-archons.md), and [voluntary spiritual practices](spiritual/practices.md).

**Try, optionally:** choose one idea (for example the Divine Spark, Veil or Archons). Describe how it could be understood as a spiritual claim *or* as symbolic language. There is **no correct mandatory declaration**. If a reflection prompt is distressing or unwelcome, skip it completely.

**Question:** What can a metaphor help someone reflect on without it becoming a diagnosis or a factual claim about the universe?

**Evidence limit:** the six Aeons are an authored spiritual sequence, not the five Sashes and not a psychological test. The Aeon Key has five modules, a separate source form.

## Session 4 — integration without inventing an institution (15–20 min)

**Original source:** `NRY-023` §§1–8, particularly §§3–6 for the difference between a proposed organization and actual status.

**Read:** [Church foundational interpretation](church.md), [whole-system relationships](SYSTEM_COHERENCE.md), and [open organizational questions](WHOLE_SYSTEM_BACKLOG.md).

**Try, optionally:** identify one way a person might study independently, one way the source imagines voluntary community, and one factual question you would need answered *before* describing an organization as operating (for example: was an entity formed, who is authorized, or where is a program held?).

**Question:** How can a document be a meaningful *foundational account of the whole* while its incorporated/ordained/operating details remain **unverified historical proposals**?

**Evidence limit:** completing a source review does not create a legal body, clergy title, membership, an instructor appointment or certification.
