# True Ascension primary-text study guide

**Sources:** OneDrive `Documents/Naijin-Ryu/True Ascension` Word collection. This document indexes the original spiritual corpus without asserting metaphysical or clinical validation.

## A Guide to True Ascension

The main guide has seven numbered divisions:

1. **The Nature of Existence:** Pleroma, creation, and the author's theory of divine fullness.
2. **The Path of the Soul:** a journey through illusion, self-knowledge and transformation.
3. **The Aeonic Path to Transcendence:** six stages from Blindness of the Flesh to Completion of the Path.
4. **The Archons:** the role of obstacles and gatekeepers in the spiritual story.
5. **Detailed Archon Profiles:** Demiurge/Yaldabaoth; Ignorance; Desire; Fear; False Revelation; Control.
6. **The Shadow Walker:** integrating difficult parts of self; the contrasting True and Dark Shadow Walker archetypes.
7. **The Final Truth:** service, collective liberation and spiritual return.

The **plain-language edition** simplifies this structure for a broader audience while explicitly encouraging a less threatening metaphorical understanding.

## Book of Signs

The source describes **seven modern signs** as social or ethical actions rather than miraculous displays:

1. Radical Witnessing
2. Sacred Containment
3. Possession Breaking (a symbolic name for questioning internalized control)
4. Abundance Reversal
5. Resurrection
6. Storm Sovereignty
7. Temple Fire

It also contains sections called **Twelve Methods of Healing**, **Protocols for Confrontation** and **Resurrection Templates**. The twelve methods belong to three source-named groups of four; see the [Book of Signs source study](book-of-signs-study.md). “Healing” is religious/reflective wording; the source does not establish clinical efficacy.

## The Aeon Key

The short guide and workbook share five modules:

1. Clear the Static
2. Come Back to Yourself
3. Anchor Your Memory
4. Release the Guilt
5. Step Outside the Noise

The workbook provides additional reflection prompts. The seven-page workbook PDF was text-compared to the Word version. Repeated page headings and quotation formatting explain some differences; full paragraph-level equivalence is not established. See [edition comparison](../source-editions.md).

## Shadow Walkers roadmap

The extended guide uses six larger sections: Cosmic and Spiritual Landscape; Aeonic Journey; Overcoming the Archons; Daily Practices and Techniques; Path to Divine Union; Final Reflections. It offers metaphor, ritual and reflection, and should not be used to diagnose a reader's mental health or spiritual advancement.

## Confrontation Scripts

The appendix contains 25 scripts for naming harmful interpersonal patterns and asserting boundaries. It offers *language options*, not proof of another person's hidden motives or a license to label disagreement as abuse.

## Archetype Integration (private)

Two nested OneDrive Word guides combine general symbolic methods with personally specific life history and intimate reflection. That private source content should not be shared in a public reference manual by default. A publication-safe **general** introduction may discuss treating archetypes as literary or reflective metaphors, but should not expose the personal examples, alleged psychological diagnoses or narrator-identity prompts.

## Reading route

Start with the plain-language guide, then the full Guide, then the Aeon Key, followed by the Book of Signs and optional Shadow Walker materials. This is an **editorial suggested order**, not a doctrinal requirement or advancement test.

The Church package accepts both literal spiritual and figurative readings and leaves martial practice optional. See [True Ascension](../true-ascension.md), [spiritual practices](practices.md) and [Church](../church.md).
