# True Ascension practice library — public conceptual index

**Sources:** `The Aeon Key.docx`, `The Aeon Key Workbook.docx`, `Book of Signs.docx`, `Confrontation Scripts Appendix.docx`, `A Guide to True Ascension.docx`, and the plain-language edition. This page intentionally excludes detailed private archetype profiles.

## The Aeon Key

The Aeon Key and workbook form an accessible set of prompts for returning attention to one's own agency. The authored module names include **Clear the Static**, **Come Back to Yourself**, **Anchor Your Memory**, **Release the Guilt**, and **Step Outside the Noise**. Some prompts invite journaling, breath awareness, remembrance and gentle reappraisal.

This is personal reflection writing, not a clinical trauma treatment or proof of any metaphysical state.

## Book of Signs

The Book of Signs frames “signs” as embodied acts rather than miraculous claims. The document's examples include **Radical Witnessing**, **Sacred Containment**, **Possession Breaking** (used figuratively for examining internalized control), and **Abundance Reversal**. The language may be spiritual or metaphorical; it should not be represented as diagnosing possession or supplying clinical care.

## Confrontation Scripts

The Appendix contains 25 patterned interpersonal scripts for identifying problematic communication and setting boundaries. They can be cataloged as **proposed communication exercises**, not definitive proof that another person is manipulating, gaslighting or acting from a diagnosable condition.

One person's experience is evidence about their experience; interpretation of another person's motives remains uncertain without corroboration.

## Shadow Walker and archetype materials

The `Complete Guide for Shadow Walkers` and the `Archetype Integration` subfolder focus on confronting and integrating inner experiences. Some nested documents describe *a named individual's* personal memories, wounds, desires, vulnerabilities and modeled “voices.” These are **private-personal reflection artifacts**. Only the general system concepts belong in public GitHub by default.

## Voluntary access and interpretation

The Church foundational package describes freedom of religious and symbolic interpretation. None of these practices should be compulsory for martial instruction, pastoral participation, or membership. Participation should never be used as a test of intelligence, loyalty, fitness or spiritual legitimacy.

## Suggested classification for future expansion

- **Doctrinal text:** source-authored narrative and symbols.
- **Accessible explanation:** plain-language interpretation.
- **Practice prompt:** nonclinical journaling or reflection.
- **Facilitator guide:** requires governance, participant consent and safeguarding.
- **Private reflection:** restricted source, not public material.
- **Empirical claim:** separate from the above; independently researched.
