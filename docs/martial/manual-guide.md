# Naijin-Ryū primary martial manual — chapter guide

**Source:** `Naijin-Ryū Manual .docx` (SHA-256 `de246912fd43…`), thirteen numbered sections, inspected directly. This is a **navigation and editorial synthesis**, not a replacement for the original source and not a technique manual.

## Critical rewritten candidate (distinct from the primary manuscript)

The complete [proposed Naijin-Ryū Martial Manual V2](../../programs/martial-training/revision-v2/NAIJIN_RYU_MARTIAL_MANUAL_V2.md), with [source-change ledger](../../programs/martial-training/revision-v2/SOURCE_CHANGE_CONTROL.md), revises interpretations of tactical sequencing, technical selection, coaching, assessment and unsupported physiological claims. This proposal is **not** the original manual, an adoption decision, a verified system or permission to reproduce restricted techniques. The chapter index below describes the historical author text, not the revised candidate.

## Chapters I–IV: foundations

**I. Core Ethos and Philosophical Grounding.** Four non-negotiable stated principles: Guard Life, Not Ego; Centerline Ascension; Shadow Integration; Skill as Sacrament. The source links practice to compassion and self-mastery.

**II. Rank Structure — Sashes of Dawn.** Charcoal, Brown, Green, Blue, White-Flare. No black belt; growth includes service, teaching and safe conduct.

**III. Technical Foundations — Five Pillars of SURGE.** Shock, Unbalance, Reversal, Grapple, Environment. The Rule of Tens limits the core technical vocabulary. The source sketches possible flow between pillars; this is not proof of real-world effectiveness.

**IV. Training Curriculum and Stages.** A developmental “Curriculum Lattice” with Foundation, Fuse, Pressure and Guardian stages, alongside a faster first-year implementation described in the **same document**. See the [normalized timetable](../curriculum.md) for how these differences have been handled with editorial authorization.

## Chapters V–IX: daily life and ethical application

**V. Daily Training Rituals and Practices.** Morning Forge and Evening Stillness; breath, deliberate movement, reflection and a Triad Journal. These are source-authored practices, not a diagnostic program.

**VI. Weekly Training Cycle.** Pillar-themed sessions Monday through Friday, Saturday conditioning/integration, Sunday rest or reflection. This is a flexible example; the early-phase four-day schedule remains possible.

**VII. Ethical Force Escalation Framework — Force Ladder.** The document advocates adapting a response to threat and prioritizing avoidance and minimum necessary force. Specific “harmless incapacitation” assertions must not be relied upon as established science; see [medical evidence review](../research-evidence.md).

**VIII. Fuel and Recovery Guidelines.** The source discusses supporting training with food, hydration, rest and recovery. This is general wellness guidance and not individualized dietary or medical direction.

**IX. Doctrine Bridge — Parallels with Aikidō’s Philosophy.** The text connects martial restraint and harmonious redirection with the Naijin-Ryū ethic. Philosophical affinity is not proof of technical lineage.

## Chapters X–XIII: community and syllabus

**X. Practical Launch Plan and Community Cultivation.** Proposes recruiting a community, training facilities and cultural rituals. Documents are plans, not proof of operating capacity, approval or supervision.

**XI. Field Mantras — Verbal Cues for Combat Flow.** Phrases serve as mnemonic and ethical cues. They are not scientific performance guarantees.

**XII. Technical Drill Syllabus — Drills by SURGE Principle.** Organized into five categories matching SURGE. The original includes contact maneuvers and improvised-weapon examples. The public index omits potentially dangerous how-to content.

**XIII. Glossary of Terms and Style Influences.** The manuscript defines martial and spiritual vocabulary and names influencing traditions. The [public glossary](../glossary.md) consolidates non-operational definitions from the separate 24-page glossary PDF and the primary manual.

## Reliability limitations

The manual's anatomical claims, rates of incapacitation, elapsed-time thresholds, asserted universality and attribution to other styles require specific independent evidence. The core ethics do not depend on treating those assertions as settled facts.
