# Naijin-Ryū — whole-system relationships and evidence boundaries

**Basis and authority:** this is an **EDITORIAL model of the author's existing work**, not a new revelation, doctrine, charter or institution. The author has explicitly identified `NRY-023` (*Church of Ascension Foundational Document Package*, §§1–2 and 7–8) as the strongest existing **collective** account. The same source's §§3–6 contain a historical possible filing/governance design. `NRY-007` supplies martial ethics, five SURGE pillars and Sashes. `NRY-017/018` supply the True Ascension spiritual framework. Sources and current operational truth are separate questions.

## Three layers that should not be confused

| Layer | Examples | What this layer establishes | What it does **not** establish |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Foundational meaning / authored commitments** | Four ethics, integration, individual conscience, discipline and service; Pleroma/Aeons as authored cosmology | The values and concepts expressed in their respective sources | Scientific proof, uniform belief, a legal corporation or obligation to participate in every activity |
| **Distinct ways of practice and study** | SURGE martial instruction; optional attention and journaling; True Ascension texts, Aeon Key and voluntary community conversation | Intended practices, alternative texts, draft curricula and editorial learning routes | Individual achievement, clinical treatment, ordained teacher authority or verified real-world effectiveness |
| **Proposed organizations and physical operations** | Historical Church charter/bylaws; youth summer camp; dojo extension and facilities | Documented intentions or plans that **might** support parts of the system | Active membership, legal filing, site rights, professional licensure, insurance, permits, staffing or a venue |

The [five Git workstreams](../programs/README.md) are **engineering coordination lanes**, *not* these three ontological layers, and not five registered institutions. The Church foundation represents the whole **at the source-interpretation level**; this does **not** make the Church workstream legally or doctrinally superior to every other workstream.

## Consent and optionality contract

The original Church package expressly permits **literal or symbolic readings** of its metaphysics and describes martial/somatic practice as **optional**. It does not require abandonment of an existing faith. Consequently an editorial path, reviewer or future program must **not** silently introduce these prerequisites:

1. Martial training as an admission condition for spiritual study, or spiritual assent as a condition for martial instruction.
2. Reported Aeonic stage or private emotional history as a technical-rank assessment.
3. Archetype disclosure or trauma narration as the price of joining a group.
4. An invented equivalence between SURGE pillars, Sashes, Aeons, Seven Signs or Aeon Key modules.
5. A required partnership with a historical but unverified corporate, clergy or physical operation.

These are **boundary interpretations grounded in source explicitness**, not extra sacred propositions; changing core authored values remains reserved to the author.

## Claim-strength and operational evidence ladder

| State | Example | Permitted language |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **USER_DIRECT** | Author confirms the Church package best describes the whole | “Author regards this as the principal umbrella text” |
| **SOURCE** | A chapter specifies an eight-week phase | “The manuscript proposes an eight-week phase” |
| **EDITORIAL** | A 32-session solo syllabus derived from broader phases | “Proposed learning sequence; not a verbatim original or approved training” |
| **EXTERNAL EVIDENCE** | Official youth-camp definition or medical research | “The external source states X; applicability to this project remains to be determined” |
| **RUNTIME / OPERATIONAL OBSERVATION** | A real class held, permit issued or instructor assessed | Only after dated, specific, relevant evidence is actually reviewed |
| **UNKNOWN** | Incorporation status, instructor credential, real learner outcomes | “Not established,” **not** “did not happen” |

Source acquisition → analyzed source → working editorial plan → verified operation are **not automatic transitions**. A public Git branch, passing test suite or source hash cannot skip any step.

## Source fidelity and remaining tensions

- **Three foundation calendars:** six-week standalone `NRY-012`, eight-week student `NRY-013`, extended lattice in `NRY-007`. The standardized eight-week calendar is **author-authorized editorial policy**, not a claim the others never existed.
- **Mental vs physical rhythms:** five themes in `NRY-009`, four Phase 1 pillar-focused training days in `NRY-013`. Editorial crosswalks must be labeled.
- **Training realism:** newer noncontact solo cards give an accessible entry but **do not cover the original's high-risk contact techniques**, partner resistance, falls or assessment under pressure. Mark the instructional gap instead of calling it “complete martial qualification.”
- **Spiritual texts:** multiple guides and Key editions overlap; exact text chronology, etymology, historical lineage and clinical effectiveness require separate evidence.
- **Plans vs activities:** the Church draft, youth camp and property proposals require separate independent status review before anyone claims present operation.
