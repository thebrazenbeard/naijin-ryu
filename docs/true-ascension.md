# True Ascension: conceptual map

**Sources:** `A Guide to True Ascension.docx`; its plain-language edition; `Naijin-Ryu, Church of Ascension – Foundational Document Package.docx`, sections 1–2.

The author's spiritual cosmology draws on Gnostic language while inviting alternative symbolic and psychological readings. **Description of doctrine is not empirical proof of the cosmology.**

## Core vocabulary

- **Pleroma:** the divine source or fullness from which existence is described as emerging.
- **Divine Spark:** a person's latent connection to that source; in nonliteral readings, a figure for interior worth or the higher self.
- **Veil of Illusion:** barriers of perception, inherited assumptions or spiritual separation.
- **Archons:** recurring obstacles or gatekeepers; the cited texts discuss Ignorance, Desire, Fear, False Revelation and Control. They may be read as entities or archetypal patterns.
- **Shadow Walker:** an individual who confronts feared, rejected or painful aspects of the self, seeking integration without domination of others.

## Six Aeons of Ascension

| Stage | Source name | Core theme |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Blindness of the Flesh | Unexamined perception |
| 2 | The Awakening Spark | Doubt and seeking |
| 3 | Recognition of Deception | Disillusionment |
| 4 | Mastery of Perception | Balance and detachment |
| 5 | Light of True Gnosis | Union with one's divine self |
| 6 | Completion of the Path | Return to unity and compassionate service |

The Church document notes that progression need not be linear. These stages are spiritual themes and **not** the five martial Sashes of Dawn, and are not a diagnostic classification.

## The accompanying reading and practice library

The True Ascension OneDrive folder holds the full Guide, a plain-language adaptation, a Book of Signs, a confrontation-script appendix, the Foundational Document Package, **The Aeon Key**, its workbook, and a Shadow Walkers guide. The subfolder **Archetype Integration** holds a guide and a formatted edition; **The Aeon Key images** holds graphic assets. Some of these sources frame reflection and journaling as practical exercises; they do not prove clinical benefit.

## Freedom of interpretation

The Foundational Document Package explicitly presents participation as voluntary and accepts literal and symbolic understanding. That feature must not be erased when creating future teaching materials.
