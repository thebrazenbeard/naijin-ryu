# Naijin-Ryū as a complete system

**Integrative authority for interpretation:** `Naijin-Ryu, Church of Ascension – Foundational Document Package.docx`, particularly sections 1–2 and 7–8. The author confirmed on 2026-10-21 that this package best describes the **collective whole**, although its Indiana incorporation text was once considered for submission and does not establish a present institutional filing.

The source's umbrella name is connected to the **Naijin-Ryū** martial path and the **True Ascension** spiritual framework. The document does not require that every participant join a church, adopt its metaphysical claims, or practice the martial art. Those distinctions are essential to the whole, not exceptions to it.

## How the domains interact

The martial ethos supplies concrete situations for care, restraint, attention and balance. The SURGE vocabulary organizes types of physical encounters without determining moral correctness. Mental practices offer ways of examining fear, attention, assumptions and choice; they do **not** guarantee particular neural or clinical effects. The Six Aeons and Archons offer spiritual or metaphorical frameworks for transformation; neither is a measured psychological diagnosis. Service and mentoring connect individual practice to community responsibility.

The key relationship is **integration without compulsory uniformity**. Naijin-Ryū's documents provide several approaches to disciplined self-development while retaining independent choices about belief and bodily practice.

## What the Church document does and does not authorize

The package is the richest umbrella **description** available from the author. The later included incorporation articles, board ideas and bylaws are **historical organizational drafts**. Neither the document nor the author clarification establishes an actual filing, legal recognition, current clergy, youth camp or operated site. Calling it a foundational *text* does not make it a presently constituted *institution*.

## How this repository represents the whole

1. Each original file remains a distinguishable source, with a stable identifier and verified hash when available.
2. Broad principles are linked to the passages that state them.
3. Variant curricula are preserved; the author has allowed the working student calendar to be normalized to eight weeks per early phase.
4. Scientific, regulatory and medical assertions are tested separately, not treated as religious doctrine.
5. Personal reflection, identifying property plans and detailed harmful procedures are kept out of the public source summaries.
6. **Ingest** receipts describe what bytes were acquired; **Roots** chronology describes possible lineage and revisions. Neither automatically establishes truth, authorship, priority or current organizational status.
