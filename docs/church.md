# Church of Naijin-Ryū — integrative foundation and historical filing draft

**Primary source:** `Naijin-Ryu, Church of Ascension – Foundational Document Package.docx`, sections 1–8.  
**Author clarification, 2026-10-21:** The package was written as material the author **considered submitting to Indiana** while exploring a business/organizational registration. It was **not established as a filed or approved application** by that statement. The author also regards the package as the **best existing description of the collective whole of Naijin-Ryū**.

## Two simultaneous functions, not competing interpretations

1. **Integrative statement of the whole.** The package draws together the martial path, its guiding ethos, True Ascension, inner integration, optional movement and contemplation, community, teaching, and the organizational vision. It is the best available high-level bridge across these domains according to the author.
2. **Historical organizational/founding draft.** The same package includes possible institutional names, Indiana nonprofit incorporation language, governance, clergy and bylaws. This records an earlier intention to explore state filing; it is **not** proof a form was submitted, a business license obtained, a corporation formed, or an institution currently operating.

The first function can inform the repository's conceptual structure **without adopting** the second as an ongoing operational plan.

## Identity, philosophy and scope

The source names **Naijin-Ryu, Church of Ascension** (with alternative names and contemplated corporate wording). Its motto is **“Integrating the Shadow, Illuminating the Self.”** It treats Naijin-Ryū as a path integrating mind, body, spirit and disciplined practice. In the religious formulation, the **Pleroma**, **Divine Spark**, **Archons**, **Six Aeons** and **Shadow Walker** frame self-knowledge and spiritual transformation.

The source also explicitly welcomes literal *or* metaphorical interpretation, and describes the martial/somatic art as **optional**. These positions preserve the difference between the broader teaching and voluntary bodily practice.

The **martial manual** details SURGE and the Sashes of Dawn. The **True Ascension guides** expand the cosmology, signs and practices. This foundational package is an **umbrella synthesis** of those bodies of material, not a replacement for their more detailed source texts.
