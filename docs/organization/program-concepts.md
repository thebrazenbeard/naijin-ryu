# Camps, dojo and practical launch concepts

**Sources:** `Camps Overview.docx`; `Naijin-Ryū Manual .docx` §X; `Naijin-Ryū Dojo Extension Build Plan.pdf`; property-specific extension proposal and floor-plan illustration (kept in their private source location).

These are **proposals** and research material, not evidence of an operating camp, an approved construction project or a licensed/insured teaching center.

## Program concepts in the camp document

The Camps Overview describes two separate seasonal **overnight programs**, each conceptualized for up to **50** participants:

| Concept | Described audience | Design elements |
|---|---|---|
| Naijin-Ryū summer camp | Children/youth | Martial arts introduction, inner-development themes, structured supervised activities |
| Fall/winter immersive horror experience | Teens/adults | Narrative experiences, controlled simulated fear and participant choice |

The second concept has substantially different psychological, consent and safeguarding risks from a youth martial arts camp. They should **not** share a one-size-fits-all intake, risk waiver or supervision plan.

## Mandatory research corrections

The camp plan's suggestion that Indiana youth camps face no state licensing/regulatory barriers is **too broad**. The Indiana Department of Health inspects qualifying youth camps and reviews construction/alteration plans under 410 IAC 6-7.2. Applicability depends on actual program duration, age, attendance, venue and other facts. See [external evidence register](../research-evidence.md).

For any youth-serving activity, participant safeguards, parental permission where applicable, background screening, incident response, qualified supervision and separate approval for physical contact or psychological stress need serious review. For a simulated horror program, clear informed consent, opt-out access without pressure, age separation and non-coercive distress response are central design requirements.

## Dojo development

The OneDrive corpus includes an extension build plan, an address-specific proposed extension and a floor-plan image. Their existence supports **design intent only**. Dimensions, costs, property ownership, approval and safe occupancy have not been verified. To avoid accidental doxxing of a home or private property, public GitHub docs omit the exact address and floor plans.
