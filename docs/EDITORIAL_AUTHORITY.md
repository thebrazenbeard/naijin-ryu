# Editorial authority and protected principles

This document describes how the public Naijin-Ryū knowledge base may evolve. It does **not** create new spiritual authority, corporate governance, rank-granting power, or ownership rights over the source manuscripts.

## Protected authored principles

The following are **core source-defined commitments** and must not be silently revised for convenience:

- **Guard Life, Not Ego:** preservation of life, restraint, refusal to indulge dominance.
- **Centerline Ascension:** the authored connection between bodily structure and inner alignment.
- **Shadow Integration:** acknowledgment and constructive integration rather than denial or coercive suppression.
- **Skill as Sacrament:** disciplined, reflective practice and service.
- The **five-pillar structure of SURGE**.
- The **five Sashes of Dawn** and deliberate absence of a black belt.
- The **six Aeons** as part of the True Ascension narrative.
- The Church package's **voluntary, universalist and non-compulsory** posture, including optional martial practice and literal/symbolic freedom.

Protected means changes require a **clear intentional author decision**, not that these claims are immune to critical interpretation or scientific/ethical review.

## Author-authorized editorial discretion

Patrick explicitly authorized non-core fixes, such as reconciling training periods, routine structure, naming equivalents and semantic consistency, while protecting core values. Accordingly the knowledge base:

- Uses eight-week Phase 1, Phase 2 and Phase 3 as the **working student calendar**.
- Retains six-week and twelve-week descriptions as source-traceable alternate pacing.
- Harmonizes **WhiteFlare / White-Flare**, **Naijin-Ryu / Naijin-Ryū**, and **Guardian / Guardian's Path / Mastery & Teaching** without misrepresenting the original spelling.
- Does not force spiritual participation as a condition of learning movement.
- Keeps **original documents unchanged**, even when a derivative page identifies an error.

## Corrections requiring no doctrinal change

Factual overstatements about medical safety, psychology, Indiana regulatory obligations, federal tax status, or achieved performance should be corrected with evidence. Such corrections are not changes to core spiritual values.

## Scope and publication approvals

Preparing review-branch documentation is permitted. Public distribution of previously private original documents, changing repository visibility, merger to `main`, claiming professional certification, creating live student records, or establishing organizational/legal authority are **separate consequential acts**, not implicit side effects of editorial permission.

## Traceability standard

For every substantial paragraph or curriculum decision, distinguish **original source statement**, **editorial normalization**, **external evidence**, and **unverified hypothesis**. Preserve the document title, hash (where known) and section/page. See [provenance](provenance.md) and [review](REVIEW.md).
