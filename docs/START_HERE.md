## The common center

The original martial manual states four ethical principles: **Guard Life, Not Ego**, **Centerline Ascension**, **Shadow Integration**, and **Skill as Sacrament**. The Church foundational package carries integration, individual worth and freedom of interpretation into the wider account. Different texts apply these ideas in **distinct registers**:

- **Martial / embodied:** five SURGE pillars and five Sashes of Dawn, with separately reviewed real-world training and safety.
- **Spiritual / reflective:** Pleroma, Divine Spark, Archons, **six Aeons**, Shadow Walker, Aeon Key and Books of Signs, available for literal or symbolic reading.
- **Community / organizational:** invitations to learning and service, alongside *historical proposals* for clergy, legal documents and activities—not proof those arrangements exist.
- **Applied programs and facilities:** optional youth education or dojo planning, each requiring its own authority and safety evidence.

These distinctions preserve the user's authored collective meaning while **not creating institutional or doctrinal dependencies** that the sources do not establish.
