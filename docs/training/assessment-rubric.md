# Assessment rubric — readiness rather than calendar rank

**Sources:** `Naijin-Ryū Manual .docx` §II, `Phases of SURGE Training Manual.docx`, with editorial harmonization under the author's permission.

## Assessment dimensions

An instructor's assessment should describe **observed behavior**, avoid diagnosing motives, and avoid equating commitment to a spiritual belief with competency in movement.

| Dimension | Not yet observed | Developing | Consistently demonstrated |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ethical understanding | Cannot articulate the no-ego and life-preservation priority | Articulates with prompting | Voluntarily chooses restraint, non-engagement and appropriate disengagement in safe scenarios |
| Movement awareness | Balance/positioning not yet repeatable | Sometimes repeatable under feedback | Repeatable in noncontact demonstration under stated conditions |
| Training partner conduct | Requires substantial supervision to honor boundaries | Responds to reminders | Communicates consent, limits, stoppage and correction reliably |
| SURGE comprehension | Cannot distinguish pillars | Identifies pillars individually | Explains their conceptual relationship without treating any sequence as compulsory |
| Reflective practice | Unable or unwilling to share (must remain optional) | Can use private reflection if desired | Can express learning goals and limitations without mandatory disclosure |
| Teaching/service (later phases) | No teaching role assigned | Observes/assists a qualified teacher | Explains safety boundaries, gives useful feedback, prevents unnecessary injury |

**Source authority:** only an actually authorized person or institution can confer a sash; a template or AI summary does not.

## Phase transition

A student may advance after safe competence is demonstrated, regardless of an abbreviated or extended calendar. Delaying advancement is a valid result. The curriculum's standard 8/8/8-week sequencing is a **teaching arrangement**, not a guarantee of physical effectiveness or eligibility for rank.

## White-Flare caution

White-Flare is the most advanced formal sash in the text and emphasizes mentorship, humility and skill; it is **not** evidence of supernatural powers, perfect fearlessness, immunity from injury, or automatic entitlement to employ lethal force. Those implications would contradict ordinary evidence standards and the system's protective ethic.

## Recordkeeping

Private assessment notes should remain appropriately access-controlled. Public GitHub can hold blank rubrics, not student identities, medical histories or spiritual confessions.
