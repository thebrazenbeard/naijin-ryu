# Review, publication, and unresolved decisions

This repository is public. The source collection includes material that should not be indiscriminately reposted, including private property plans, historical personal health references, prospective legal documents and a detailed last-resort force manual. The present review branch adds **source-based synthesis and navigation**, not verbatim copies of those materials.

## Evidence labels

- **SOURCE:** text actually recorded in a named source document.
- **DOCTRINE:** belief, value, symbolism or authored conceptual model.
- **PROPOSAL:** prospective curriculum, activity, institution or facility.
- **INFERENCE:** editorial comparison or organizing judgment.
- **UNKNOWN:** claim not established from available evidence.
- **VERIFIED:** independent observation with appropriate method and traceable result.

A document's technical or medical explanation is a source assertion, not an independent finding.

## Editorial invariants

1. Keep source text and editorial summary separately identifiable.
2. Preserve contradictory versions without quietly selecting one.
3. Don't equate a belief claim with a scientific observation.
4. Don't treat a design plan as approval, construction or operational existence.
5. Do not republish private or injurious operational detail as a default.
6. Record exact document, edition, headings and later page locators for important claims.

> **HOSTILE REVIEWER:** Could orderly documentation imply a settled, proven school when the files contain conflicting schedules and untested efficacy claims?

**Accepted.** Organizing the corpus does not validate its technical, scientific or legal claims. Unresolved evidence is shown explicitly, and editorial notes remain revisable.
