# Phase 1, Week 4 — Consolidate and recover

**Source** (NRY-013): four Phase 1 themed days — Shock, Unbalance, Reversal, Grapple; Environment is observed throughout. **EDITORIAL:** this page is a proposed solo week, not a transcription of NRY-013's higher-risk contact sequences, an instructor-approved safety protocol, or evidence of advancement. Use only comfortable noncontact motion and existing [progression gates](phase-1-progression-gates.md); repeat earlier work whenever needed. No medically relevant personal details belong in this public course.

**Readiness question:** Can explain a stop rule and report when a movement is not yet ready; no forced advancement.

## A — Shock: Slow technical checkpoint (30 min)

| Clock | Task | Exact solo instructions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0–5 min** | Preparation | Walk or march easily; use comfortable shoulder/hip motions and find a clear practice lane. |
| **5–11 min** | Footwork | Alternate small forward, backward and side steps. Reset guard and balance after each movement. |
| **11–19 min** | Pillar practice | Repeat your preferred Week 2 straight-line footwork sequence at roughly half your usual pace. Add optional light open-hand movements only if fully comfortable; prioritize relaxed returns over repetitions. |
| **19–26 min** | Cardio | Three 1-minute comfortably brisk / 1-minute easy walk or march pairs, then a final optional comfortable brisk minute; reduce pace as needed. |
| **26–30 min** | Review | Walk slowly; check technique, effort and recovery; record only in a private log. |

**Simplify if needed:** Footwork alone counts.

**What to observe, not claim as a credential:** Can you describe one control detail and one reason to stop?

## B — Unbalance: Support-assisted stance review (25 min)

| Clock | Task | Exact solo instructions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0–5 min** | Preparation | Easy walking and comfortable ankle/hip range near stable support. |
| **5–12 min** | Base control | Practice forward/back and lateral stance changes without crossing feet or rushing. |
| **12–17 min** | Pillar practice | Use a stable chair as a safety reference while repeating gentle forward/back and lateral weight shifts. Break the practice into comfortable sets with generous pauses. |
| **17–22 min** | Conditioning | Easy or brisk walking according to the talk test and balance; pause if unstable. |
| **22–25 min** | Review | Walk easily; record base and recovery observations privately. |

**Simplify if needed:** Remain in a natural standing stance with ordinary walking.

**What to observe, not claim as a credential:** Can you reset your base without compensating through the upper body?

## C — Reversal: Pause-before-turn review (26 min)

| Clock | Task | Exact solo instructions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0–5 min** | Preparation | Easy walking and gentle, comfortable turning. |
| **5–13 min** | Direction changes | Slow small pivots with a full balance reset before the next repetition; both directions. |
| **13–19 min** | Pillar practice | From a quiet stance, look at a clear travel path, say 'pause', make one low-angle pivot, stop, then choose whether another step is necessary. |
| **19–23 min** | Conditioning | Conversational-pace walking or marching; no speed target. |
| **23–26 min** | Review | Slow down; note the quality of decisions and recovery privately. |

**Simplify if needed:** Look-and-point response with no pivot.

**What to observe, not claim as a credential:** Can you decline movement when there is no safe path?

## D — Grapple: Safety vocabulary check (24 min)

| Clock | Task | Exact solo instructions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0–5 min** | Preparation | Walk and check clear, nonslip floor, safe routes and available stable supports. |
| **5–11 min** | Spacing | Step between comfortable positions, retain balance, and notice obstructions without touching a partner. |
| **11–16 min** | Pillar practice | At an easy pace, alternate a comfortable stance, one step away and a verbal 'stop'. Spend the remainder identifying a safe floor and explaining why an untrained fall or submission is excluded. |
| **16–21 min** | Conditioning | Easy walking or marching with optional pauses. |
| **21–24 min** | Review | Record stopping, spacing and recovery observations privately. |

**Simplify if needed:** Use ordinary walking and verbal choices alone.

**What to observe, not claim as a credential:** Can you explain the training limits without demonstrating a risky technique?

## Week 4 decision gate

Can explain a stop rule and report when a movement is not yet ready; no forced advancement. **If missing or unclear, repeat or reduce the session rather than automatically moving to Week 5.** New pain, instability, or concerning symptoms call for stopping the affected activity and appropriate qualified advice. No forced disclosure, high-intensity striking, throws, locks, ground submissions or surprise threats. All training remains optional and adaptable.
