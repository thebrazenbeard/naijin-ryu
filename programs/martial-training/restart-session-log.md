# Blank training log — restart and progression review

**Purpose:** a privacy-safe format for tracking a returning student's first solo workouts and deciding what to review next. **Evidence class: EDITORIAL.** Inspired by the repository's [session log](../../docs/training/session-templates.md) and safety-first [assessment rubric](../../docs/training/assessment-rubric.md), not a new rank scheme.

**Important:** Copy this blank template into a **private** record. Do not commit completed logs containing a person's symptoms, injury history, personal reflections, identity or health information to this public repository.

```text
Session date:
Week / day (A, B, C, D):
Planned theme / session:
Actual minutes:
What I performed or modified:
Overall effort (0–10, self-rated):
Comfort or unexpected symptoms during activity:
Recovery later that day / next morning:
Breathing and footwork control:
One technical detail to retain:
One change for the next session:
Assessment status: practiced / not completed / needs qualified review
```

## Next-session decision (editorial framework)

**Repeat** when the person is still learning basic coordination, wants another baseline, or cannot reliably judge technique. **Reduce** intensity/duration when fatigue, discomfort or balance problems occur. **Progress modestly** only if the preceding work was comfortable, next-day recovery was acceptable and movement control remained reliable. **Stop/refer** when concerning new or persistent symptoms appear or a relevant professional's advice conflicts with the proposed session.

The instructor assessment ladder stays separate: **planned → attended → practiced → assessed → qualified**. A private self-report is useful for training decisions, but it is neither objective certification nor evidence of supernatural, clinical or combat outcomes.
