## 3. What has NOT yet been rewritten/qualified

- No attempt has been made to reconstruct all original contact combinations from private source pages; doing so would make this a speculative transcript and could publish high-risk instructions.
- The actual **complete named technique list, ranked by candidate/core/restricted/retired**, is **not** yet independently assembled from NRY-007 §XII and NRY-013's drills. The [test standard](TECHNIQUE_AND_TEST_STANDARD.md) defines how to do that without assuming success.
- No external instructor, athlete or biomechanics expert has reviewed the V2 movement instructions.
- No medical, educational or safety organization has certified Naijin-Ryū or the rewritten program.
- NRY-016 contains source prose and stance diagrams; exact illustrations are **not being modified** by this PR, and their generated replacement art should not be mistaken for qualified biomechanics.
- NRY-010 contains historical personalized rehabilitation milestones; current individual medical capacity cannot be inferred from those timings.
- The science in the revised manual is cited in [Research and Claims](RESEARCH_AND_CLAIMS.md), but the studies are largely about **related activities**, not SURGE outcome trials.
- No original source content, personal health/trauma log or exact property/site plans have been released into GitHub.

## 4. Meaning of “rewrite” and document priority

**ORIGINAL/AUTHORED:** the unmodified NRY sources define *what Patrick originally authored*. They keep their historical identity even if a subsequent version disagrees with a factual statement.

**V2 PROPOSED:** these new documents define *how an evidence-sensitive operational edition could be written*. They are substantive, usable revision proposals—not source quotations. They should not be silently labeled “Official Original Manual” or treated as independent approval.

**SUPPLEMENTARY:** short 25–30-minute return-to-practice cards in PR #5 are **later generic or noncontact derivative workouts**. They do not replace the original detailed technical curriculum and must never be used to pretend it has been fully taught.

**ADOPTION:** a future author decision could explicitly accept some/all C01–C12 and publish an adopted edition, with version history and separately qualified physical procedures. Merge to `main` would be a distinct technical/governance act, and is not requested or implied by saving this GitHub draft.

It **preserves** source-defined families, day allocations, rank architecture, contact-training objectives and criteria for responsibly developing the complete library. It refuses to invent private text, assert efficacy, or turn risk into authority. The unresolved distance between technical descriptions and trained capability is clearly recorded.

**No one yet.** The request to draft a rewrite authorizes work on a review branch. It does not automatically establish adoption, staff appointment, compliance or real-world ability. Every proposal remains labeled pending the author's substantive decision and appropriate external review.
