# Phase 1 — instructor observation and feedback protocol

**Status:** **EDITORIAL ASSESSMENT AID**, source-linked but **not** an established Naijin-Ryū credential, official grading policy, professional standard, or training license. Source `NRY-007` (§§I–IV) describes the ethical principles, five Sashes of Dawn and SURGE fundamentals; `NRY-013` provides the four-day Phase 1 structure. This protocol deliberately assesses what can reasonably be **observed in a safe introductory setting** rather than pretending a solo course demonstrates contact techniques.

## Competence is not the same as attendance

For each observation mark one of:
- **Observed with support:** learner completed a safe version with coaching or a stable aid.
- **Observed independently:** learner performed a stated *noncontact* task reliably without unsafe hurry.
- **Not yet demonstrated:** attempted under reasonable conditions but not consistently controlled.
- **Not assessed:** a task was not tested; this is **missing evidence**, not a failing grade.

Record **task conditions**, **assessor identity and actual authority** (if any), **modifications**, and **next safe learning step** in a controlled private system. Only blank templates belong in public Git. An unauthorised observer may document what they saw; they may not confer sash or instructor status.

## Sample observation tasks (editorial)

| Domain | Benign demonstration | Observe for | What remains out of scope |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Guard Life, Not Ego** | Learner describes two reasons *not* to enter an avoidable confrontation | Restraint, recognition of bystanders, call for help or disengagement | Real threat psychology or legal advice |
| **Shock — self-control** | Unhurried stance-and-optional-open-hand movement in clear space | Retains own balance, relaxed return to guard, stops for discomfort | Impact mechanics and striking efficacy |
| **Unbalance — own base** | Supported weight shifts and short step-to-reset tasks | Stable support, controlled recovery, no unsafe lean | Off-balancing, sweeping or throwing a partner |
| **Reversal — direction choice** | Chooses a clear exit and makes a small controlled pivot or ordinary turn | Checks footing, pauses and changes plans safely | Counters, joint locks or resistance work |
| **Grapple — space and boundaries** | Maintains personal-space distance, uses verbal stop and steps away | Honors stop immediately and does not grasp or restrain | Clinch, escapes, breakfalls and ground holds |
| **Environment — awareness** | Identifies a blocked route, an open route and uninvolved people in a verbal scenario | Correctly distinguishes observed facts from guesswork; prefers safety | Verified performance under real attack |
| **Recovery and self-correction** | Reviews a low-intensity session and names a limitation | Can reduce, repeat or stop rather than escalate to impress | Medical diagnosis or therapeutic monitoring |

## A safe sample review meeting (approximately 20 minutes, editorial)

1. **2 minutes — boundaries:** explain tasks and permission to omit any drill.
2. **5 minutes — movement basics:** one stable stance and controlled steps; no contact or surprise threat.
3. **5 minutes — pillar examples:** learner chooses short low-risk movements representing whichever two pillars are comfortable today.
4. **5 minutes — judgment:** talk through fictional choices: leave, ask for help, respect consent, avoid bystanders.
5. **3 minutes — feedback:** observer states what was actually seen, what was not assessed, and one next learning option.

The timings do not establish an official examination length. Coaching/assessment of original *contact* curriculum requires separate qualified instructors, facilities, consent and appropriate safety plans; these noncontact tasks cannot fill that gap.

## Ethical and epistemic gates

Do not use physiological claims from the manuals as performance tests. Do not demand sacred doctrine, an Aeon identification, personal trauma disclosures or a reflection diary as a condition of martial learning. The source's **Sashes of Dawn** remain distinct from the **Six Aeons** and from this editorial checklist.

> **HOSTILE REVIEWER:** Could a structured rubric convey a false impression of institutional examiner authority or make a noncontact practice look like complete martial instruction?

**Accepted.** Every observation task here is scoped to introductory *noncontact* behavior. The record cannot award rank, and the limitations column explicitly names the contact skills it does **not** assess. A real instructional qualification would need source-faithful supervised physical training and independently justified safety and evaluation methods.
