# Phase 4 — Guardian's Path: mentorship and continuing practice

**Source scope:** `NRY-013`, *Phases of SURGE Training Manual.docx*, Part II, “Mastery Training Manual – Continuous Evolution,” “Phase 4 Overview: The Guardian’s Path (Months 1–6+)”, “Teaching Apprenticeship” and “Philosophical and Spiritual Integration.” Related: `NRY-007`, manual §IV and sash principles. **Status:** authored teaching progression with editorial competence/safeguarding gates.

## Role described by the source

Phase 4 is **continuing development**, not a six-month endpoint at which mastery is proven. The source combines more exacting refinement of the five SURGE pillars with **helping others learn**. It proposes that a Guardian begin as a supervised student-teacher, receive feedback, teach a limited part of a class, and grow into mentorship. The manuscript associates this stage with humility, non-dominance and possible spiritual reflection.

A named **Guardian** stage is **not** an instructor license, employment contract, rank award or independently confirmed certification. The **White-Flare Sash** is a separate authored rank; this document does not automatically confer it.

## The source's apprenticeship outline

| Manuscript period | Described teaching role | Editorial verification needed before any real appointment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Months 1–2** | Assistant alongside a more experienced instructor; observation, warm-up guidance and simple corrections | Existing teaching authority, observation of communication and safeguarding, never unaccompanied minors |
| **Months 3–4** | Plan and lead a limited lesson segment under feedback | Demonstrated explanation, consent, age-appropriate activity choices, immediate stopping and correction |
| **Months 5–6 and beyond** | Continued refinement, more independent planning and community service | Repeated observations and authorization scoped to the actual group and tasks; annual or event-specific reassessment where needed |

Only the **periods and general apprenticeship trajectory** are drawn from the manuscript. The rightmost safeguards are **editorial proposals**, not established operating practice. The source does not prove that an organization currently has a master instructor or staffed apprenticeship.

## Suggested public assessment dimensions

| Domain | Observable question |
| --- | --- |
| **Ethical modeling** | Does the Guardian model “Guard Life, Not Ego,” including stopping rather than rewarding aggression? |
| **Clarity of explanation** | Can they explain a principle in learner-appropriate language without requiring advanced physical risk? |
| **Partner and student autonomy** | Do they seek consent, respond to refusal without pressure, and avoid mandatory spiritual disclosure? |
| **Learning feedback** | Can they identify an error without shaming, overclaiming expertise or inventing physiological explanations? |
| **Limits and escalation** | Do they call a qualified supervisor when a task, physical condition or safeguarding concern exceeds their role? |
| **Continuing study** | Do they document uncertain knowledge and revise teaching methods when evidence disagrees with an assertion? |

Use [blank assessment templates](../../docs/training/assessment-rubric.md) and keep **actual student, safeguarding and health records private**. Never treat an automated evaluation or a favorable narrative as authorization to teach.

## Optional spiritual parallels

The source invokes *A Guide to True Ascension* and the **Shadow Walker** as reflective parallels. Those are **source-authored worldview elements** and do not make religious belief an admission requirement for martial instruction. The integrative Church package treats martial practice as optional as well. In a secular training class, mentorship and restraint remain assessable without asserting metaphysical truth.

## Publication and readiness boundaries

The manuscript's high-risk advanced physical techniques remain in the private source, not in public novice instructions. Before any actual program, independent review would need instructor credentials, youth safeguarding where applicable, appropriate emergency and facilities controls, and explicit author/institutional authority for appointments. The manuscript is an aspirational blueprint; none of those external conditions is verified solely by its text.
