# Phase 1, Week 7 — Consistency under self-selected variation

**Source** (NRY-013): four Phase 1 themed days — Shock, Unbalance, Reversal, Grapple; Environment is observed throughout. **EDITORIAL:** this page is a proposed solo week, not a transcription of NRY-013's higher-risk contact sequences, an instructor-approved safety protocol, or evidence of advancement. Use only comfortable noncontact motion and existing [progression gates](phase-1-progression-gates.md); repeat earlier work whenever needed. No medically relevant personal details belong in this public course.

**Readiness question:** Can reproduce stable movement on two separate attempts, with consistent stopping and environmental attention.

## A — Shock: Choose and repeat two sequences (30 min)

| Clock | Task | Exact solo instructions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0–5 min** | Preparation | Walk or march easily; use comfortable shoulder/hip motions and find a clear practice lane. |
| **5–11 min** | Footwork | Alternate small forward, backward and side steps. Reset guard and balance after each movement. |
| **11–19 min** | Pillar practice | Select two comfortable noncontact footwork patterns from prior weeks. Repeat each at slow pace, then revisit the easier one at the same pace; do not increase power for a 'final test'. |
| **19–26 min** | Cardio | Three 1-minute comfortably brisk / 1-minute easy walk or march pairs, then a final optional comfortable brisk minute; reduce pace as needed. |
| **26–30 min** | Review | Walk slowly; check technique, effort and recovery; record only in a private log. |

**Simplify if needed:** Repeat only one familiar step pattern.

**What to observe, not claim as a credential:** Was movement repeatable without unnecessary shoulder tension?

## B — Unbalance: Stability comparison (25 min)

| Clock | Task | Exact solo instructions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0–5 min** | Preparation | Easy walking and comfortable ankle/hip range near stable support. |
| **5–12 min** | Base control | Practice forward/back and lateral stance changes without crossing feet or rushing. |
| **12–17 min** | Pillar practice | Choose two previously practiced supported weight shifts. Alternate them while keeping a steady base, then describe which requires further practice rather than pushing for longer holds. |
| **17–22 min** | Conditioning | Easy or brisk walking according to the talk test and balance; pause if unstable. |
| **22–25 min** | Review | Walk easily; record base and recovery observations privately. |

**Simplify if needed:** Practice with both feet on the floor and a stable support.

**What to observe, not claim as a credential:** Can you recognize the less reliable pattern without concealing it?

## C — Reversal: Exit choice explanation (26 min)

| Clock | Task | Exact solo instructions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0–5 min** | Preparation | Easy walking and gentle, comfortable turning. |
| **5–13 min** | Direction changes | Slow small pivots with a full balance reset before the next repetition; both directions. |
| **13–19 min** | Pillar practice | Imagine two possible exits; identify an obstruction in one and choose the other. Walk only the unobstructed path in a cleared area, return slowly, and re-evaluate. |
| **19–23 min** | Conditioning | Conversational-pace walking or marching; no speed target. |
| **23–26 min** | Review | Slow down; note the quality of decisions and recovery privately. |

**Simplify if needed:** Make a verbal choice without walking.

**What to observe, not claim as a credential:** Do the reasons for choosing an exit remain consistent?

## D — Grapple: Boundary and stop rehearsal (24 min)

| Clock | Task | Exact solo instructions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0–5 min** | Preparation | Walk and check clear, nonslip floor, safe routes and available stable supports. |
| **5–11 min** | Spacing | Step between comfortable positions, retain balance, and notice obstructions without touching a partner. |
| **11–16 min** | Pillar practice | Practice a comfortable stance, a calm verbal boundary, and a small backward/side step. Repeat without raising volume, force, or physical threat simulation. |
| **16–21 min** | Conditioning | Easy walking or marching with optional pauses. |
| **21–24 min** | Review | Record stopping, spacing and recovery observations privately. |

**Simplify if needed:** Verbal boundary and ordinary walk only.

**What to observe, not claim as a credential:** Can you stop without tension, escalation or reliance on another person?

## Week 7 decision gate

Can reproduce stable movement on two separate attempts, with consistent stopping and environmental attention. **If missing or unclear, repeat or reduce the session rather than automatically moving to Week 8.** New pain, instability, or concerning symptoms call for stopping the affected activity and appropriate qualified advice. No forced disclosure, high-intensity striking, throws, locks, ground submissions or surprise threats. All training remains optional and adaptable.
