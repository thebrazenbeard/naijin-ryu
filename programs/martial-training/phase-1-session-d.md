# Phase 1 — Session D: Grapple principles without contact

**Source:** `NRY-013`, Phase 1 “Day 4: Grapple Training – Clinch & Ground Control Fundamentals.” The original includes clinch, takedown, ground-control and submission material that **cannot be safely replicated from a generic public solo document**. This EDITORIAL restart substitutes spatial awareness, posture and noncontact repositioning. A solo 24-minute session is **not grappling instruction or certification**.

**Target:** approximately **24 minutes**, no partner, throws, ground submissions, joint locks, chokes or untrained falling practice. A clear level floor is sufficient.

| Time | Segment | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0–5 min** | Prepare and scan | Gentle walk and mobility. Confirm the surface is clear, stable and not slippery, and identify available help and an unobstructed exit. |
| **5–11 min** | Distance and position | Slowly step between two imagined points, stopping in a stable stance. Notice where hands, feet and nearby obstacles are, without contacting or restraining anything. |
| **11–16 min** | Recovery and disengagement | From a comfortable stance, practice stepping out of a confined imaginary space and returning to relaxed posture. A supported sit-to-stand can be substituted **only if already comfortable and individually appropriate**. |
| **16–21 min** | Comfortable conditioning | Five minutes of continuous or broken-up easy walking/marching, with pauses as needed. |
| **21–24 min** | Reset and assessment | Note whether you retained balance, could stop promptly and recognized when the exercise exceeded your present comfort. |

## What this teaches—and does not

This is a **preparatory awareness session** for the Grapple pillar, not a learned throw, breakfall, escape from an opponent, ground hold, or self-defense system. Under an appropriately trained instructor, the source's contact techniques would require separate instruction, safeguards and consent. No public solo plan grants permission to perform them.

No independent martial outcome follows from completing four sessions. This day may be repeated, reduced or replaced with walking if fatigue, balance or other limitations make it more appropriate.

**Optional mental theme:** `NRY-009` Day 4 pairs Grapple and Environment in awareness under pressure. Editorial prompt: *Did I notice a safe exit before trying to solve the imaginary constraint?*
