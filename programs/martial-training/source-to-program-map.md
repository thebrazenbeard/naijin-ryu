## Course expansion, not a new primary source

The [full eight-week Phase 1 solo course](phase-1-eight-week-course.md) and its [structured registry](../../data/training-phase1-eight-week-v1.json) provide 32 editorial noncontact sessions. They preserve **NRY-013**'s four day-pillar identities and week count, but deliberately replace the manuscript's contact techniques with low-risk solo positioning, aerobic and decision-making exercises. The [instructor observation aid](phase-1-instructor-observation.md) cannot certify the source's throws, locks, strikes, ukemi or ranks. **These additions are not direct source quotations.**

## What the public plans change (editorial only)

The original Phase 2 and Phase 3 descriptions prescribe increasingly complex techniques and physical pressure. **These derivative public plans deliberately substitute noncontact, consent-based learning objectives** where replicating original procedures would create undue risk. This is an editorial safety filter, **not** a claim that the original manuscript itself contained every substitute.

The three eight-week student phases come from **NRY-013** and Patrick's accepted calendar normalization. The **NRY-007** Curriculum Lattice is slower, and its concrete first-year implementation is faster than the broad lattice. The sources must not be silently averaged into a fictitious original timetable.

The mental-conditioning source `NRY-009` describes body/brain mechanisms and symbolic transformation. Its assertions about the vagus nerve, trauma processing, reaction time, hormones and neural changes are not established by merely appearing in the source; no therapeutic or medical outcome is promised.

## Open verifications

- A definitive **page-by-page Word/PDF edition comparison** for NRY-013 and NRY-014, including layout/images, has not been completed here.
- Contact instruction, qualified supervision, public liability arrangements, physical outcomes, youth-safeguarding capacity and actual rank authority remain unverified operational conditions.
- Student-specific diaries, mental-health records, instructor or child identities and restricted last-resort combat material must never be put into public GitHub.
- A verified *source phrase* establishes wording in a hashed document. It does not by itself verify truth, section accuracy, pedagogical suitability, or correct historical attribution.
