# Phase 1, Week 8 — Review, not graduation

**Source** (NRY-013): four Phase 1 themed days — Shock, Unbalance, Reversal, Grapple; Environment is observed throughout. **EDITORIAL:** this page is a proposed solo week, not a transcription of NRY-013's higher-risk contact sequences, an instructor-approved safety protocol, or evidence of advancement. Use only comfortable noncontact motion and existing [progression gates](phase-1-progression-gates.md); repeat earlier work whenever needed. No medically relevant personal details belong in this public course.

**Readiness question:** Any advancement requires an authorized instructor's separate practical assessment; solo completion is not evidence of contact skill or rank.

## A — Shock: Shock baseline repeat (30 min)

| Clock | Task | Exact solo instructions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0–5 min** | Preparation | Walk or march easily; use comfortable shoulder/hip motions and find a clear practice lane. |
| **5–11 min** | Footwork | Alternate small forward, backward and side steps. Reset guard and balance after each movement. |
| **11–19 min** | Pillar practice | Repeat the safest Week 1 or Week 2 footwork/optional air-motion work at comparable effort. Do not intentionally push past baseline. Note whether returns to stance appear controlled. |
| **19–26 min** | Cardio | Three 1-minute comfortably brisk / 1-minute easy walk or march pairs, then a final optional comfortable brisk minute; reduce pace as needed. |
| **26–30 min** | Review | Walk slowly; check technique, effort and recovery; record only in a private log. |

**Simplify if needed:** Only stance/footwork is sufficient.

**What to observe, not claim as a credential:** Which observable detail changed, and what remains unknown?

## B — Unbalance: Unbalance baseline repeat (25 min)

| Clock | Task | Exact solo instructions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0–5 min** | Preparation | Easy walking and comfortable ankle/hip range near stable support. |
| **5–12 min** | Base control | Practice forward/back and lateral stance changes without crossing feet or rushing. |
| **12–17 min** | Pillar practice | Repeat a short supported weight shift and a simple stance reset. If possible, compare with Week 2 using the same support and difficulty without forcing longer holds. |
| **17–22 min** | Conditioning | Easy or brisk walking according to the talk test and balance; pause if unstable. |
| **22–25 min** | Review | Walk easily; record base and recovery observations privately. |

**Simplify if needed:** Ordinary walking, no balance challenge.

**What to observe, not claim as a credential:** Is the movement reproducible rather than just 'felt better'?

## C — Reversal: Reversal baseline repeat (26 min)

| Clock | Task | Exact solo instructions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0–5 min** | Preparation | Easy walking and gentle, comfortable turning. |
| **5–13 min** | Direction changes | Slow small pivots with a full balance reset before the next repetition; both directions. |
| **13–19 min** | Pillar practice | Choose a small side step/pivot and safe exit. Move slowly, check footing, then explain why disengagement may be preferable to confrontation. |
| **19–23 min** | Conditioning | Conversational-pace walking or marching; no speed target. |
| **23–26 min** | Review | Slow down; note the quality of decisions and recovery privately. |

**Simplify if needed:** Verbal route choice and no pivot.

**What to observe, not claim as a credential:** Can you make a reasoned non-engagement choice?

## D — Grapple: Grapple boundary demonstration (24 min)

| Clock | Task | Exact solo instructions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0–5 min** | Preparation | Walk and check clear, nonslip floor, safe routes and available stable supports. |
| **5–11 min** | Spacing | Step between comfortable positions, retain balance, and notice obstructions without touching a partner. |
| **11–16 min** | Pillar practice | Demonstrate spacing, calm 'stop', safe retreat and identification of the limits of solo practice. Do not attempt throws, holds, submissions or ukemi as a test. |
| **16–21 min** | Conditioning | Easy walking or marching with optional pauses. |
| **21–24 min** | Review | Record stopping, spacing and recovery observations privately. |

**Simplify if needed:** Use a verbal assessment and gentle walking.

**What to observe, not claim as a credential:** Can you clearly distinguish practiced concepts from instructor-verified technique?

## Week 8 decision gate

Any advancement requires an authorized instructor's separate practical assessment; solo completion is not evidence of contact skill or rank. **If missing or unclear, repeat or reduce the session rather than claiming completion or advancing into Phase 2.** New pain, instability, or concerning symptoms call for stopping the affected activity and appropriate qualified advice. No forced disclosure, high-intensity striking, throws, locks, ground submissions or surprise threats. All training remains optional and adaptable.
