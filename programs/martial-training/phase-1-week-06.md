# Phase 1, Week 6 — Adapt to benign information changes

**Source** (NRY-013): four Phase 1 themed days — Shock, Unbalance, Reversal, Grapple; Environment is observed throughout. **EDITORIAL:** this page is a proposed solo week, not a transcription of NRY-013's higher-risk contact sequences, an instructor-approved safety protocol, or evidence of advancement. Use only comfortable noncontact motion and existing [progression gates](phase-1-progression-gates.md); repeat earlier work whenever needed. No medically relevant personal details belong in this public course.

**Readiness question:** Can pause on an unexpected but nonthreatening cue and choose safe space without rushing.

## A — Shock: Change the travel lane (30 min)

| Clock | Task | Exact solo instructions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0–5 min** | Preparation | Walk or march easily; use comfortable shoulder/hip motions and find a clear practice lane. |
| **5–11 min** | Footwork | Alternate small forward, backward and side steps. Reset guard and balance after each movement. |
| **11–19 min** | Pillar practice | Choose one of two clear walking lanes before each set. Step toward the chosen lane, stop, and make only a slow noncontact arm motion if comfortable. Alternate the lane between sets. |
| **19–26 min** | Cardio | Three 1-minute comfortably brisk / 1-minute easy walk or march pairs, then a final optional comfortable brisk minute; reduce pace as needed. |
| **26–30 min** | Review | Walk slowly; check technique, effort and recovery; record only in a private log. |

**Simplify if needed:** Use just walking and hand-free guard observation.

**What to observe, not claim as a credential:** Can you change plans without adding speed or contact?

## B — Unbalance: Footing observation (25 min)

| Clock | Task | Exact solo instructions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0–5 min** | Preparation | Easy walking and comfortable ankle/hip range near stable support. |
| **5–12 min** | Base control | Practice forward/back and lateral stance changes without crossing feet or rushing. |
| **12–17 min** | Pillar practice | Without introducing actual hazards, verbally imagine slick versus clear flooring. Explain which route you'd avoid; practice small movements only on a verified safe surface. |
| **17–22 min** | Conditioning | Easy or brisk walking according to the talk test and balance; pause if unstable. |
| **22–25 min** | Review | Walk easily; record base and recovery observations privately. |

**Simplify if needed:** Do the decision-making portion seated.

**What to observe, not claim as a credential:** Does caution override the urge to finish repetitions?

## C — Reversal: Stop-and-redirect cue (26 min)

| Clock | Task | Exact solo instructions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0–5 min** | Preparation | Easy walking and gentle, comfortable turning. |
| **5–13 min** | Direction changes | Slow small pivots with a full balance reset before the next repetition; both directions. |
| **13–19 min** | Pillar practice | Choose a neutral spoken cue such as 'change'. During slow stepping, pause when you say the cue yourself, check balance, then choose a different safe direction. Never use surprise attacks. |
| **19–23 min** | Conditioning | Conversational-pace walking or marching; no speed target. |
| **23–26 min** | Review | Slow down; note the quality of decisions and recovery privately. |

**Simplify if needed:** Use a full stop between every step, with no direction change.

**What to observe, not claim as a credential:** Did you pause and recheck footing before redirecting?

## D — Grapple: Personal-space and permission (24 min)

| Clock | Task | Exact solo instructions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0–5 min** | Preparation | Walk and check clear, nonslip floor, safe routes and available stable supports. |
| **5–11 min** | Spacing | Step between comfortable positions, retain balance, and notice obstructions without touching a partner. |
| **11–16 min** | Pillar practice | In an imagined queue or crowded hallway, choose a path that respects other people's space and allows you to leave. Walk it slowly on an empty floor and say 'stop' as a rehearsal of boundaries. |
| **16–21 min** | Conditioning | Easy walking or marching with optional pauses. |
| **21–24 min** | Review | Record stopping, spacing and recovery observations privately. |

**Simplify if needed:** Describe the route without moving.

**What to observe, not claim as a credential:** Can you explain consent and personal-space limits?

## Week 6 decision gate

Can pause on an unexpected but nonthreatening cue and choose safe space without rushing. **If missing or unclear, repeat or reduce the session rather than automatically moving to Week 7.** New pain, instability, or concerning symptoms call for stopping the affected activity and appropriate qualified advice. No forced disclosure, high-intensity striking, throws, locks, ground submissions or surprise threats. All training remains optional and adaptable.
