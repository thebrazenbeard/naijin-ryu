# Phase 1, Week 2 — Repeatable base

**Source** (NRY-013): four Phase 1 themed days — Shock, Unbalance, Reversal, Grapple; Environment is observed throughout. **EDITORIAL:** this page is a proposed solo week, not a transcription of NRY-013's higher-risk contact sequences, an instructor-approved safety protocol, or evidence of advancement. Use only comfortable noncontact motion and existing [progression gates](phase-1-progression-gates.md); repeat earlier work whenever needed. No medically relevant personal details belong in this public course.

**Readiness question:** Can repeat each basic pattern smoothly and stop before overbalancing.

## A — Shock: Line and reset (30 min)

| Clock | Task | Exact solo instructions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0–5 min** | Preparation | Walk or march easily; use comfortable shoulder/hip motions and find a clear practice lane. |
| **5–11 min** | Footwork | Alternate small forward, backward and side steps. Reset guard and balance after each movement. |
| **11–19 min** | Pillar practice | Mark an imaginary line. Step to it, extend a light open-hand motion in empty air if comfortable, then step back to guard. Try 4 unhurried sets with a reset between; no target or contact. |
| **19–26 min** | Cardio | Three 1-minute comfortably brisk / 1-minute easy walk or march pairs, then a final optional comfortable brisk minute; reduce pace as needed. |
| **26–30 min** | Review | Walk slowly; check technique, effort and recovery; record only in a private log. |

**Simplify if needed:** Hands may remain at guard and only the steps are practiced.

**What to observe, not claim as a credential:** Does the stance remain stable on the return?

## B — Unbalance: Balanced weight shift (25 min)

| Clock | Task | Exact solo instructions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0–5 min** | Preparation | Easy walking and comfortable ankle/hip range near stable support. |
| **5–12 min** | Base control | Practice forward/back and lateral stance changes without crossing feet or rushing. |
| **12–17 min** | Pillar practice | Within arm's reach of a stable support, shift weight to one foot and back through center, then the other. Repeat at a slow pace without leaning beyond support. |
| **17–22 min** | Conditioning | Easy or brisk walking according to the talk test and balance; pause if unstable. |
| **22–25 min** | Review | Walk easily; record base and recovery observations privately. |

**Simplify if needed:** Use both feet flat and a much smaller shift.

**What to observe, not claim as a credential:** Did you deliberately recover your base before the next move?

## C — Reversal: One-step turn (26 min)

| Clock | Task | Exact solo instructions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0–5 min** | Preparation | Easy walking and gentle, comfortable turning. |
| **5–13 min** | Direction changes | Slow small pivots with a full balance reset before the next repetition; both directions. |
| **13–19 min** | Pillar practice | Take one small step away from an imagined obstacle, turn the torso and feet together comfortably, pause, then return. Alternate right and left. |
| **19–23 min** | Conditioning | Conversational-pace walking or marching; no speed target. |
| **23–26 min** | Review | Slow down; note the quality of decisions and recovery privately. |

**Simplify if needed:** Remove the turn and practice a side step and stop.

**What to observe, not claim as a credential:** Was any turn rushed or unstable?

## D — Grapple: Distance and disengage (24 min)

| Clock | Task | Exact solo instructions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0–5 min** | Preparation | Walk and check clear, nonslip floor, safe routes and available stable supports. |
| **5–11 min** | Spacing | Step between comfortable positions, retain balance, and notice obstructions without touching a partner. |
| **11–16 min** | Pillar practice | Choose two harmless floor reference points. From a comfortable stance step to create extra space, stop, and calmly walk toward the clear exit. Repeat without speed. |
| **16–21 min** | Conditioning | Easy walking or marching with optional pauses. |
| **21–24 min** | Review | Record stopping, spacing and recovery observations privately. |

**Simplify if needed:** Choose fewer steps or simply point to the available exit.

**What to observe, not claim as a credential:** Could you leave instead of approaching the imagined constraint?

## Week 2 decision gate

Can repeat each basic pattern smoothly and stop before overbalancing. **If missing or unclear, repeat or reduce the session rather than automatically moving to Week 3.** New pain, instability, or concerning symptoms call for stopping the affected activity and appropriate qualified advice. No forced disclosure, high-intensity striking, throws, locks, ground submissions or surprise threats. All training remains optional and adaptable.
