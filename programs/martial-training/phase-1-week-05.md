# Phase 1, Week 5 — Link two safe ideas

**Source** (NRY-013): four Phase 1 themed days — Shock, Unbalance, Reversal, Grapple; Environment is observed throughout. **EDITORIAL:** this page is a proposed solo week, not a transcription of NRY-013's higher-risk contact sequences, an instructor-approved safety protocol, or evidence of advancement. Use only comfortable noncontact motion and existing [progression gates](phase-1-progression-gates.md); repeat earlier work whenever needed. No medically relevant personal details belong in this public course.

**Readiness question:** Can link simple movements without losing control; connection is conceptual, not a contact combination.

## A — Shock: Footwork to noncontact motion (30 min)

| Clock | Task | Exact solo instructions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0–5 min** | Preparation | Walk or march easily; use comfortable shoulder/hip motions and find a clear practice lane. |
| **5–11 min** | Footwork | Alternate small forward, backward and side steps. Reset guard and balance after each movement. |
| **11–19 min** | Pillar practice | Step sideways, return to stance, then extend a gentle palm motion into empty space if comfortable; immediately reset without force or rush. Repeat in loose, self-selected sets. |
| **19–26 min** | Cardio | Three 1-minute comfortably brisk / 1-minute easy walk or march pairs, then a final optional comfortable brisk minute; reduce pace as needed. |
| **26–30 min** | Review | Walk slowly; check technique, effort and recovery; record only in a private log. |

**Simplify if needed:** Use the step and return with hands relaxed.

**What to observe, not claim as a credential:** Does the hand motion interrupt balance or shoulder comfort?

## B — Unbalance: Center to direction (25 min)

| Clock | Task | Exact solo instructions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0–5 min** | Preparation | Easy walking and comfortable ankle/hip range near stable support. |
| **5–12 min** | Base control | Practice forward/back and lateral stance changes without crossing feet or rushing. |
| **12–17 min** | Pillar practice | Shift weight gently, recover to centered stance, then take a small step away from a marked boundary. Pause between elements to avoid uncontrolled momentum. |
| **17–22 min** | Conditioning | Easy or brisk walking according to the talk test and balance; pause if unstable. |
| **22–25 min** | Review | Walk easily; record base and recovery observations privately. |

**Simplify if needed:** Split the two actions into separate exercises.

**What to observe, not claim as a credential:** Can you clearly tell when you have returned to your base?

## C — Reversal: Pivot to exit (26 min)

| Clock | Task | Exact solo instructions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0–5 min** | Preparation | Easy walking and gentle, comfortable turning. |
| **5–13 min** | Direction changes | Slow small pivots with a full balance reset before the next repetition; both directions. |
| **13–19 min** | Pillar practice | Make a small comfortable pivot and then, only if stable, take two walking steps toward a clear exit. Repeat after resetting and choosing the other side. |
| **19–23 min** | Conditioning | Conversational-pace walking or marching; no speed target. |
| **23–26 min** | Review | Slow down; note the quality of decisions and recovery privately. |

**Simplify if needed:** Perform an ordinary walking turn without a pivot.

**What to observe, not claim as a credential:** Was the selected exit clear before taking the first step?

## D — Grapple: Space to stop (24 min)

| Clock | Task | Exact solo instructions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0–5 min** | Preparation | Walk and check clear, nonslip floor, safe routes and available stable supports. |
| **5–11 min** | Spacing | Step between comfortable positions, retain balance, and notice obstructions without touching a partner. |
| **11–16 min** | Pillar practice | At two imagined floor points, move from a closer to a farther safe distance, stop on command, and state whether a clear exit is available. No partner resistance or ground work. |
| **16–21 min** | Conditioning | Easy walking or marching with optional pauses. |
| **21–24 min** | Review | Record stopping, spacing and recovery observations privately. |

**Simplify if needed:** Walk normally while simply naming distances.

**What to observe, not claim as a credential:** Was disengagement the first solution rather than control of another person?

## Week 5 decision gate

Can link simple movements without losing control; connection is conceptual, not a contact combination. **If missing or unclear, repeat or reduce the session rather than automatically moving to Week 6.** New pain, instability, or concerning symptoms call for stopping the affected activity and appropriate qualified advice. No forced disclosure, high-intensity striking, throws, locks, ground submissions or surprise threats. All training remains optional and adaptable.
