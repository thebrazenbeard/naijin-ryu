# Phase 1, Week 3 — Controlled directional variety

**Source** (NRY-013): four Phase 1 themed days — Shock, Unbalance, Reversal, Grapple; Environment is observed throughout. **EDITORIAL:** this page is a proposed solo week, not a transcription of NRY-013's higher-risk contact sequences, an instructor-approved safety protocol, or evidence of advancement. Use only comfortable noncontact motion and existing [progression gates](phase-1-progression-gates.md); repeat earlier work whenever needed. No medically relevant personal details belong in this public course.

**Readiness question:** Can maintain balance and comfortable breathing when movement directions change.

## A — Shock: Four-direction square (30 min)

| Clock | Task | Exact solo instructions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0–5 min** | Preparation | Walk or march easily; use comfortable shoulder/hip motions and find a clear practice lane. |
| **5–11 min** | Footwork | Alternate small forward, backward and side steps. Reset guard and balance after each movement. |
| **11–19 min** | Pillar practice | Imagine a small square. Step forward, sideways, backward, sideways, returning to the start. Keep hands relaxed or in a comfortable guard; add only a slow noncontact hand extension if desired. |
| **19–26 min** | Cardio | Three 1-minute comfortably brisk / 1-minute easy walk or march pairs, then a final optional comfortable brisk minute; reduce pace as needed. |
| **26–30 min** | Review | Walk slowly; check technique, effort and recovery; record only in a private log. |

**Simplify if needed:** Shrink the square or remove the hand movement.

**What to observe, not claim as a credential:** Did the steps remain small enough for stable footing?

## B — Unbalance: Clock-face steps (25 min)

| Clock | Task | Exact solo instructions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0–5 min** | Preparation | Easy walking and comfortable ankle/hip range near stable support. |
| **5–12 min** | Base control | Practice forward/back and lateral stance changes without crossing feet or rushing. |
| **12–17 min** | Pillar practice | From a central stance, take short steps toward 12, 3, 6 and 9 o'clock, returning to center after each. Use a handrail or chair if needed. |
| **17–22 min** | Conditioning | Easy or brisk walking according to the talk test and balance; pause if unstable. |
| **22–25 min** | Review | Walk easily; record base and recovery observations privately. |

**Simplify if needed:** Use only two directions, or perform seated foot-placement awareness.

**What to observe, not claim as a credential:** Did the center feel equally recoverable after each direction?

## C — Reversal: Two-choice path (26 min)

| Clock | Task | Exact solo instructions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0–5 min** | Preparation | Easy walking and gentle, comfortable turning. |
| **5–13 min** | Direction changes | Slow small pivots with a full balance reset before the next repetition; both directions. |
| **13–19 min** | Pillar practice | Imagine a clear doorway on either side. Name the safer option based on footing and bystanders, take two unhurried steps toward it, and stop; repeat after switching the imagined surroundings. |
| **19–23 min** | Conditioning | Conversational-pace walking or marching; no speed target. |
| **23–26 min** | Review | Slow down; note the quality of decisions and recovery privately. |

**Simplify if needed:** Decide and point without turning if movement is uncomfortable.

**What to observe, not claim as a credential:** Did the decision precede movement rather than follow a rush?

## D — Grapple: Stable retreat (24 min)

| Clock | Task | Exact solo instructions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0–5 min** | Preparation | Walk and check clear, nonslip floor, safe routes and available stable supports. |
| **5–11 min** | Spacing | Step between comfortable positions, retain balance, and notice obstructions without touching a partner. |
| **11–16 min** | Pillar practice | Begin at a safe distance from a wall. Practice two backward-then-side steps, stopping well before any obstacle. Return by walking normally; do not roll, fall or kneel. |
| **16–21 min** | Conditioning | Easy walking or marching with optional pauses. |
| **21–24 min** | Review | Record stopping, spacing and recovery observations privately. |

**Simplify if needed:** Use a single sideways step and stop.

**What to observe, not claim as a credential:** Was there a clear route with no contact or collisions?

## Week 3 decision gate

Can maintain balance and comfortable breathing when movement directions change. **If missing or unclear, repeat or reduce the session rather than automatically moving to Week 4.** New pain, instability, or concerning symptoms call for stopping the affected activity and appropriate qualified advice. No forced disclosure, high-intensity striking, throws, locks, ground submissions or surprise threats. All training remains optional and adaptable.
