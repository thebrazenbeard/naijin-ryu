# Immersive horror program

**Work branch:** `program/horror-immersion` · **Integrated authority:** [`main`](../../README.md) · **Workstream status:** CONCEPT-ONLY

The original camp proposal also describes a **distinct** fall/winter immersive horror experience for teens and adults. This is a separate design lane rather than a seasonal variation of the youth martial camp: participant age, consent, staged fear, staff training and psychological safety require different controls.

**Primary public-indexed sources:** NRY-001 and NRY-002, the same proposed-program study. Source reuse does not merge the two participant programs.

**Existing guides:** [original program-concepts comparison](../../docs/organization/program-concepts.md), [public safety and regulatory research](../../docs/research-evidence.md).

**Next research/development:** conceptualize explicit informed consent, real-time opt-outs, safe physical boundaries, noncontact stage direction, participant age separation, and independent review of distress/trauma risks. Establish an appropriate legal/insurance review only if a real venue and dates are ever proposed.

**Boundary:** no unconsented fear conditioning, deceptive real-world threats, simulated injury without safeguards, private attendee data or claim of an operating attraction. No public launch occurs from drafting alone.
