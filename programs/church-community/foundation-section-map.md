# Church foundation package — eight-section evidence map

**Primary source:** `NRY-023`, *Naijin-Ryu, Church of Ascension – Foundational Document Package.docx*, sections 1–8. **Source status:** historical authored package, not a recorded government filing or approved community charter. The author identifies it as the strongest existing **umbrella description** of Naijin-Ryū as a whole.

## Eight source sections and their distinct roles

| Original section | What is actually in the manuscript | Public treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Formal Name and Identity** | Proposed Church of Ascension names; motto **Integrating the Shadow, Illuminating the Self**; universalist participation | **SOURCE:** philosophy and provisional naming; *not* a registered or currently used legal name |
| **2. Core Doctrine Statement** | Pleroma / Divine Spark; Archons; Six Aeons; Shadow Walkers; multiple interpretations; optional somatic/martial practice | **SOURCE / DOCTRINE:** integrated authored worldview, not independent cosmological or clinical evidence |
| **3. Governance and Leadership** | Proposed founder leadership, emerging community teachers, directors and checks on authority | **HISTORICAL PROPOSAL:** no existing board, elected officers or active clergy established |
| **4. Clergy and Ordination Model** | Companion/mentor rather than divine intermediary; service, honesty, confidentiality, apprenticeship and possible recognition | **HISTORICAL PROPOSAL:** neither ordination nor counseling license follows from the prose |
| **5. Articles of Incorporation Language (Indiana Nonprofit)** | Suggested corporate name, purpose, nonprofit protections, director and membership clauses | **DRAFT:** no submission, approval, nonprofit registration, tax exemption or current legal status verified |
| **6. Bylaws Outline** | Possible membership, directors/officers, spiritual leadership, meetings, conflict resolution and amendments | **DRAFT:** outlines choices rather than enacted rules, operational authority or member voting rights |
| **7. Archetype Integration Framework** | Individualized symbolic inner-parts framework, optional reflection and sensitive autobiographical examples | **DOCTRINE / PRIVATE:** describe the general model only; founder-specific personal examples remain withheld |
| **8. Spiritual Practices Overview** | Aeon Key, contemplation, journaling, optional movement and collective practices, with a voluntary posture | **SOURCE:** conceptual library, not evidence that ceremonies, groups or services are currently held |

## Principle that governs the whole

The first two sections make room for **literal or metaphorical** accounts of the Divine Spark, Archons and Aeons. They expressly allow people of other faiths or of no asserted doctrinal conformity to participate voluntarily. Section 2 calls physical/martial movement an **optional** expression of the spiritual framework, not an initiation requirement. An individual who does not practice Naijin-Ryū martial techniques is not therefore disqualified from the spiritual concept.

The package also uses **Naijin-Ryu** to name spiritual doctrine and **Naijin-Ryū** as the martial art. The shared naming is original-source usage; it is not permission to collapse all spiritual or organizational commitments into a mandatory martial curriculum.

## How to interpret Sections 3–6

The manuscript uses declarative and future-tense statements about a founder, clergy, a board, Indiana legal formation, and bylaws. A plan can be written *as though* the institution has already been formed. That is a writing convention, **not evidence that formation occurred**. At present only the historical *proposal* is supported by this source.

A later real organization would require **independent documentation** of its status, bylaws, governance appointments, tax position and relevant professional requirements. Some proposed legal assertions in the manuscript may be wrong or superseded; they must be independently checked before use. This derivative does not offer filing instructions.

## Section 7 privacy rule

The original includes highly personal founder narratives and named inner archetypes. They explain where the author developed the framework but are not automatically public biographical data. This map intentionally records **only the general principle** that symbolic self-models are personal, change over time, and should not be imposed on other people. It does not reproduce private autobiographical examples, diagnoses or supposed internal voices.

## What this map does *not* claim

This document does not establish present clergy, active members, a legal church, an operating facility, a certified somatic school, authenticated spiritual experiences, or professional clinical treatment. It records authored doctrine and historical plans with a section-by-section boundary.
