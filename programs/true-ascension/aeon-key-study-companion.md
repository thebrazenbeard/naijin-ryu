# The Aeon Key — five-module reading and reflection companion

**Public source basis:** `NRY-029`, *The Aeon Key.docx*, short five-module guide; `NRY-027`, *The Aeon Key Workbook.docx*, extended reflection format; `NRY-028`, separate seven-page PDF edition. **Classification:** source-based study guide with editorial questions, not a replacement for source originals or treatment advice.

## The two forms of the Key

The **short Key** outlines five practical moments of self-reflection. The **Word workbook** retains the five module names while adding context, optional writing prompts, blank note space and an introductory invitation to use the toolkit in any order. The PDF workbook is a separately ingested object; textual overlap is imperfect, and a full layout/paragraph comparison remains open. We therefore **do not declare all three byte-identical or semantically identical editions**.

| Original module name | What the short guide emphasizes | Additional feature in the workbook | Optional editorial question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Clear the Static** | Question whether inner criticism reflects one's own convictions or borrowed beliefs | Prompts about recurring thoughts, when they were learned, and alternative understandings | Which assumption could I examine without having to replace it immediately? |
| **2. Come Back to Yourself** | Reconnection with an earlier or neglected sense of self | Guided younger-self reflection and space for notes | What could present-day care for myself look like in a small, concrete action? |
| **3. Anchor Your Memory** | Write a simple reminder of personal identity and meaning | Dedicated daily completion and memory-anchor prompts | What value matters to me today, even if my feelings change? |
| **4. Release the Guilt** | Reconsider past choices with the understanding then available | Expanded prompts on learning, self-forgiveness and new perspective | What have I learned without needing to excuse harm or demand reconciliation? |
| **5. Step Outside the Noise** | Use an accessible calming ritual or environment change | Several grounding suggestions and writing about the world one wants to inhabit | Which low-pressure activity would help me attend to my immediate surroundings? |

**Evidence key:** module names, relative emphases and presence of the workbook prompt spaces are **SOURCE**. The rightmost reflection questions are **EDITORIAL** additions, not direct quotations from the author.

## Use without turning reflection into an obligation

The workbook explicitly permits sections to be used **in any order and at any pace**. Journaling, imagery and spoken statements are optional. A reader may decline to discuss memories or explain an experience to a facilitator. A record of an exercise is not evidence of psychological improvement, mystical status, medical efficacy or suitability for a particular relationship.

Some modules invite recollection of younger versions of self, guilt, loss or difficult experience. A facilitator must not demand personal disclosure or claim to provide trauma therapy. Where a prompt becomes distressing, it is appropriate to stop, change topics or seek qualified support. Reflection is not a diagnostic method.

## The Key is not the Six Aeons

The corpus also contains the **Six Aeons** in the True Ascension full guide and a **Seven Signs** collection in the Book of Signs. Five Key modules, six spiritual stages, seven ethical Signs and twelve titled reflective methods are **distinct organizations of different texts**. Treating the five modules as five Aeons or five martial sashes would create a false equivalence.
