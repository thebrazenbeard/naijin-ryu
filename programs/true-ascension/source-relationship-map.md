## Numbers are not ranks

- **Five Sashes of Dawn:** authored martial progression, not a spiritual scale.
- **Six Aeons:** phases of the True Ascension spiritual account, not a diagnostic test or martial credential.
- **Five Aeon Key modules:** tools for optional reflection, not five spiritual levels.
- **Seven Signs:** outward ethical or symbolic behaviors, explicitly framed as **not magic** in the original.
- **Twelve methods:** source heading *Twelve Methods of Healing*, split into three groups; neither medical treatments nor the Six Aeons.
- **Twenty-five confrontation scripts:** rhetorical options, not evidence of another person's intent or condition.

## Optional readings and privacy

Literal faith and symbolic reflection are different legitimate readings **within the corpus**, and the Church package's description does not impose physical training. Conversely, martial competence should not be graded by professed belief or private reflections. The nested archetype-integration manuscripts include personally specific material, so their text is **not** reproduced or mined for public-facing case studies.

The exact chronological authorship of ideas across the corpus remains **UNKNOWN**. The current data include preserved files and hashes, not a complete authenticated authoring timeline. A thematic resemblance is evidence of **similarity** and nothing stronger without additional independent proof.
