## Proposed verification progression

1. **First, source custody:** restore authorized access to the general `NRY-011` PDF and verify it against the existing private intake hash. Inspect text and layout; avoid exposing restricted neighboring files or addresses.
2. **Then, a redacted source review:** extract high-level functional suggestions and identify any historical estimates. Mark any speculative costs, engineering terms or drawings as unverified until independently checked.
3. **Next, define program needs:** distinguish the actual martial training scope from optional spiritual meetings and youth camps. Include activity, access, supervision and safeguarding assumptions.
4. **Only for a real site, conduct professional review:** use qualified parties and current applicable official requirements to determine construction, safety, zoning, use and occupancy pathways.
5. **Before any public announcement:** check actual authority to build/use the premises and to operate the proposed activities; retain sensitive property records outside the public repo.

Every step is a **future proposed gate**, not evidence that it has happened. A Git commit, training outline, or private sketch cannot stand in for a permit or an engineer.

## Safety and privacy invariants

No public address, exact floor plan, site vulnerabilities, property-owner biography, personal medical information or protected student record is needed to draft a functional brief. Any true location-specific drawings or risk assessments require private authorized custody, not a public `program/*` branch.

The original PDF's apparent title is not enough to conclude that a building, drawing, approval or bid exists. Until direct source review succeeds, no more specific statement about its contents should be added to the public reference.
