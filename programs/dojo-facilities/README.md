# Dojo and facilities development

**Work branch:** `program/dojo-facilities` · **Integrated authority:** [`main`](../../README.md) · **Workstream status:** CONCEPT-ONLY

This workstream covers the **proposed** Naijin-Ryū teaching space and associated facility needs. Existing sources include a general extension/build-plan estimate and separate property-specific drawings that must remain private. The work here can address abstract room functions, accessibility, sanitation, safeguarding and public permitting research without identifying a site.

**Primary public-indexed source:** NRY-011, a general build-plan PDF. Other property- and floorplan-bearing originals remain restricted and are not reproduced or individually identified here.

**Existing guides:** [dojo development overview](../../docs/organization/program-concepts.md), [public legal/safety evidence](../../docs/research-evidence.md), [program safeguarding](../youth-camp/README.md).

**Next research/development:** produce location-neutral functional and safety requirements, identify what building/fire/zoning review would be necessary for a **specific** future project, and distinguish materials estimates from professional cost and occupancy approvals.

**Boundary:** no private property address, floor plan, site photographs or owner-identifying details on public work branches; no assertion of permission to construct, occupancy, inspection or actual facility operation.
