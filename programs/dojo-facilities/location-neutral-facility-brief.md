## Functional questions for a future teaching space

| Area or feature | Functional question to resolve | Evidence needed before a real design decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Movement/activity zone** | What low-impact learning activity, number of students, and instructor-to-participant supervision will actually be supported? | Instructor-approved curriculum, capacity concept, professionally prepared layout |
| **Accessible arrival and circulation** | Can people of differing mobility access relevant activities and emergency routes? | Site-specific accessibility review, inclusive participation needs, applicable codes |
| **Safe surface and obstacle management** | Are footing, transitions, edges, furnishings, ventilation and lighting appropriate for the permitted activities? | Professional risk and facility assessment; equipment selection and maintenance plan |
| **First-response capability** | How would staff identify and respond to injuries or emergencies? | Responsible operators, training, emergency services access, site-specific plan |
| **Private reception/administration** | Where would necessary consent and registration processes occur without making personal data public? | Restricted records/process design and access controls; no actual identities in Git |
| **Storage and equipment** | Can training equipment be stored without exposing children or visitors to hazards? | Equipment inventory, supervision and physical safety review |
| **Restroom, hydration and sanitation** | What facilities are required for realistic occupancy and duration? | Professional/local authority determination; water, plumbing and cleaning plan |
| **Community/quiet use** | Can voluntary discussion or reflection occur without pressuring attendees into religious practices? | Separate operational scope and published voluntary-participation policy |

These are **design questions**, not an instruction to build any specific room or a claim that the current source PDF supplies such dimensions.

## Program separation

A dojo's function must be specified separately from a youth camp, a proposed Church meeting space, or the **unrelated** immersive horror idea. Shared ownership or building plans cannot be inferred from a historical collection of files. A facility proposal does not create an organization, credential or approval for any activity.
