# Naijin-Ryū — Inner-God Style

An authored martial, ethical, and spiritual framework structured around **Guard Life, Not Ego**, the **five SURGE pillars** (Shock, Unbalance, Reversal, Grapple, Environment), **Sashes of Dawn**, and **True Ascension**. Spiritual participation and physical training are distinct, and the proposed Church of Ascension explicitly describes martial practice as optional.

This repository is being organized as a source-traceable knowledge base. Its documents may express spiritual beliefs, proposals, or scientific/medical/technical claims; none should be confused with independently verified fact.
